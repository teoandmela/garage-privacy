Safety first

Bulino's results are a calculation aid, not a guarantee: responsibility for the machining stays with the person running the machine. Always follow the tool manufacturer's data and the machine's safety rules. When in doubt, start from the more cautious values.

Frequently asked questions

How do I read the tool code?

In Calculate, tap the camera icon to the right of the code field, then Take a photo or Choose a photo. Frame the marking on the box or the tool up close, straight and in good light.

Bulino shows I read this: check it, correct it if needed and tap Use this code. The app uses only the code you confirm. It reads, for example, insert codes (CNMG 120408-PM), threads (M10, M12x1.5, 1/4-20 UNC, G 1/2) and markings on drills and end mills (Ø 8.5 carbide, D12 Z3).

The reading is wrong or finds no code. What should I do?

Get closer, add light, avoid reflections and try again. Or type the code by hand in the field: the calculation works just the same. If Bulino doesn't recognize the code, it uses the data you enter in the fields below.

Why are they “starting values”?

Bulino calculates with public formulas and with ranges we wrote by comparing tool manufacturers' guides. But every machine, setup, tool and material is different. Start from these values and adjust them by watching the chips, the sound and the finish. If your tool manufacturer's catalog says otherwise, follow the catalog.

How does it respect my machine's limits?

In Machines, tap New machine and enter Maximum speed and Spindle power; if you know them, also add Minimum speed, Maximum torque, Maximum cutting feed, Speed limit with G96 for lathes, and Rigidity. Bulino keeps the values within these limits. Without a machine, the values don't take speed and power into account.

Millimeters or inches?

At first Bulino chooses based on your phone's country. You can change it any time in Settings › Units: Millimeters or Inches.

How do recipes work?

After a calculation, tap Save recipe: you can note how it went, the parts made, a note and a photo. Next time, for a similar job with the same material or the same tool, Bulino suggests It already worked: with Use these values you start again from what worked, adapted to today's machine and diameter. In Recipes you can search them by material, tool, machine or note.

What is the part counter for?

In Shop floor mode you see the values in large type, the screen stays on and you count parts. When you change the edge, tap I changed the edge: Bulino learns how many parts an edge makes and uses it in later estimates.

What does I have a problem do?

After a calculation, tap I have a problem and choose what you see, for example Chatter. Bulino shows the likely causes and corrected values; Apply uses them right away.

Where is my data saved?

Only on this phone, in Bulino's storage. There are no accounts and no online copies: we can't see your data and can't recover it. Details: Privacy policy.

What happens if I change phones?

Bulino has no account and no sync. Your data can move to the new phone only if the system transfers it, with a backup or a phone-to-phone transfer (iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android). We can't guarantee it, because Apple and Google manage it. Data doesn't move between iPhone and Android: before switching, write down your most important recipes.

How do I change the language?

Bulino uses your phone's language. In Settings › Language you'll find the button Change it in the phone's settings, which opens the phone's settings.

How do I delete all my data?

Tap the gear icon at the top to open Settings, then Delete all data, and confirm with Delete everything. Recipes, photos, machines, counts and settings are removed from this phone, and it cannot be undone.
