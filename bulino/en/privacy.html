Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Bulino helps people who work at machine tools: speed, feed and depth of cut from the tool, shop formulas, machining recipes with photos, tool comparison with PDF, and a part counter. Here we explain, in plain words, what happens to your data.

In short

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No data reaches us or anyone else: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app doesn't use the internet: it calculates and saves everything on your phone.
  • You can delete everything whenever you like.

What Bulino saves, and where

Bulino saves only on your phone, in the app's storage: the machines you add, your recipes with notes and photos, part counts and settings such as units and theme.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Internet

Bulino has no server and doesn't connect to the internet: calculations, formulas and code reading work on the phone, even with no connection. On Android the app doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

Camera and photos

The camera opens only when you ask: to read the tool code or to photograph the part for a recipe.

Text is read on the phone, without the internet: on iPhone and iPad with Apple's built-in system features (Vision), on Android with a model that lives inside the app (Google ML Kit). The photo taken to read the code is deleted right after reading, and the app uses only the code you confirm.

Choose a photo opens your phone's photo picker, and Bulino receives only the photo you pick. Recipe photos are copied into the app's storage and stay there until you delete the recipe.

If you don't allow camera access, you can always type the code by hand.

Licences, courses and expiry dates

If you use the section for licences and courses, Bulino saves on your phone the certificates you enter, with dates and issuers, and any photos or PDFs of certificates you add. For the occupational medical check it keeps only the date of the next visit: no assessment and no health data. This data also stays only on your phone.

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone, without the internet. Permission for notifications is requested only when you turn them on.

The record PDF is created on the phone: you choose the entries, whether to include your name and the certificate photos. You share it only if you want.

This data, with its attachments and reminders, is also removed with Settings › Delete all data.

The comparison PDF

The tool comparison PDF is created on the phone. You share it only if you want, with the phone's share sheet, and you choose who gets it. From then on, the rules of the app you send it with apply.

Deleting your data

You can delete a recipe or a machine at any time. Settings › Delete all data removes recipes, photos, machines, counts and settings from this phone; it cannot be undone.

If you uninstall the app, its data is removed from the phone too.

Phone backups

If you have iCloud or Google backup turned on, the system may include Bulino's data in the backup. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under their own terms: we can't see it or use it.

If you write to us

If you contact support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to reply, and we delete them when they're no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

You have the rights granted by privacy laws, such as the GDPR in Europe: to know what data we hold and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If Bulino ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version of the app.
